## Accessibility

<A11yCallout>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Root element.</strong> Renders as a native <code>&lt;output&gt;</code>,
      whose implicit ARIA role genuinely is <code>status</code> — no explicit{" "}
      <code>role</code> attribute needed.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Dismiss button.</strong>{" "}
      <code>role=&quot;button&quot;</code> with{" "}
      <code>aria-label=&quot;Remove&quot;</code>, and its own{" "}
      <code>glow-focus</code> ring.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Dot indicator.</strong> Purely decorative — <code>aria-hidden</code>.
    </li>
  </ul>
</A11yCallout>
